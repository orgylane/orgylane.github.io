how i made it

Originally, I wanted a sequel to my last album "Ethernet Teas", but I realised I don't want that vibe anymore... I mean, it's good, but I overdid it and got bored. Then came the idea, to mix my other persona, MiKMiKO into teasbogre... That made me realise: I love producing silly stuff. If you've listened to the self-titled MiKMiKO album you know the vibe. So I tried and tried and in a week, I had all the beats for the album. Especially the "PROJECT_?" ones. Then I just wrote some lyrics (they took more time then the beats LOL) and done! I also got a lil bored of FL Studio, so track 2, "andY" was a live recording session in Ableton. The uncut, 2 hour footage can be found on my Youtube! (as soon as i can upload that long ass video)
